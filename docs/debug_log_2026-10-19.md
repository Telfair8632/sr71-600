## 1. Firmware: Level 6 `level6_pwm` (complete, verification pending)

- Created `firmware/level6_pwm/`: four channels of 50 Hz **hardware** PWM — GP6/GP7 servos at 1500 µs, GP8/GP9 ESCs at 1000 µs; sys_clk integer-divided to a 1 MHz counter, wrap = 19999, period exactly 20.000 ms.
- Anti-glitch power-up sequence (for the acceptance items "no startup glitch / reset-safe"): immediately after reset all four outputs plus GP2 are strongly driven low with internal pull-downs enabled → PWM starts at 0 % duty → self-test runs ~0.5 s (pins held low throughout) → only after the self-test passes are the pins switched to the PWM function; on self-test failure they stay low permanently. GP2 / OE gate defaults low at power-up.
- Serial commands: `s` channel-identification sweep (1250/1750/1100/1150 µs, 2 s each, to catch miswired channels), `c` re-center, `e`/`d` OE gate on/off, `w` watchdog reset test.
